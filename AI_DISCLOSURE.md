# AI ASSISTANCE DISCLOSURE

**Project:** SONIX: The Earth Information Jukebox  
**Team:** Team SONIX  
**NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2026**

---

## 1. Transparency & Use of AI Tools
In accordance with NASA International Space Apps Challenge guidelines, Team SONIX openly discloses that **Antigravity (by Google DeepMind)**, an agentic AI coding assistant, and AI image generation tools were used to assist during the hackathon development cycle.

---

## 2. Scope of AI Assistance

| Category | How AI Was Used | Human Verification & Originality Guarantee |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Visual Assets (Logo)** | Generated the SONIX project emblem using an AI image generation model. | The emblem is transparently marked "Logo: AI-generated" across the application, About modal, and documentation. |
| **Code Implementation** | Assisted in drafting boilerplate TypeScript interfaces, Three.js shader scaffolding, and Web Audio API node connection graphs. | All architecture, sound design rules, math formulas, and component logic were specified and reviewed by Team SONIX. Tested with automated unit tests. |
| **Data Ingestion Scripting** | Generated Node.js stream download and CSV parsing syntax for `scripts/fetch-data.mjs`. | All URLs, headers, and parsing rules were manually cross-checked against the official NASA GISS GISTEMP v4 documentation. Every data point was verified against NASA records. |
| **Documentation & Narrative Drafting** | Assisted in drafting initial outlines for submission narrative sections and demonstration video scripts. | All scientific claims, numbers, citations, and limitation statements were audited and verified by team members against NASA primary literature. |

---

## 3. Affirmation of Scientific Honesty & Data Integrity
Team SONIX affirms:
- **No data was generated, interpolated, hallucinated, or randomized by AI.** All 146 annual temperature anomaly records originate directly from the NASA Goddard Institute for Space Studies.
- **The sonification mapping is an exact mathematical function, not an AI-generated musical composition.**
- All third-party software, datasets, and fonts are cited accurately in [REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md).
