## 1. Scientific Data Sources

### NASA Goddard Institute for Space Studies (GISS)
- **Dataset:** NASA GISS Surface Temperature Analysis (GISTEMP v4), Global Land-Ocean Temperature Index (GLB.Ts+dSST.csv)
- **Time Range:** 1880 to 2025 (146 consecutive years analyzed)
- **Baseline Period:** 1951–1980 global mean
- **Source URL:** https://data.giss.nasa.gov/gistemp/tabledata_v4/GLB.Ts+dSST.csv
- **Primary Citations:**
  1. GISTEMP Team, 2024: *GISS Surface Temperature Analysis (GISTEMP), version 4.* NASA Goddard Institute for Space Studies. Dataset accessed November 2024 at https://data.giss.nasa.gov/gistemp/.
  2. Lenssen, N., G. Schmidt, J. Hansen, M. Menne, A. Perkins, R. Ruedy, and D. Shindell, 2019: *Improvements in the GISTEMP uncertainty model.* J. Geophys. Res. Atmos., 124, no. 12, 6307–6326, doi:10.1029/2018JD029522.
  3. Hansen, J., R. Ruedy, M. Sato, and K. Lo, 2010: *Global surface temperature change.* Rev. Geophys., 48, RG4004, doi:10.1029/2010RG000345.
- **License / Terms of Use:** Public Domain (NASA open data policy / U.S. Government Work).
