1. SCIENTIFIC DATASETS:
- NASA GISS Surface Temperature Analysis (GISTEMP v4): Global Land-Ocean Temperature Index (GLB.Ts+dSST.csv), 1880–2025.
  URL: https://data.giss.nasa.gov/gistemp/tabledata_v4/GLB.Ts+dSST.csv
  Citation: GISTEMP Team, 2024: GISS Surface Temperature Analysis (GISTEMP), version 4. NASA Goddard Institute for Space Studies. Dataset accessed November 2024 at https://data.giss.nasa.gov/gistemp/.
  Secondary Citation: Lenssen, N., G. Schmidt, J. Hansen, M. Menne, A. Perkins, R. Ruedy, and D. Shindell, 2019: Improvements in the GISTEMP uncertainty model. J. Geophys. Res. Atmos., 124, no. 12, 6307–6326, doi:10.1029/2018JD029522.
  License: Public Domain (U.S. Government Work).

2. NASA VISUALIZATIONS & EARTH INFORMATION CENTER:
- NASA Earth Information Center (EIC): Visual frames and climate indicators.
  URL: https://earth.gov and https://earth.gov/explore
- NASA Scientific Visualization Studio (SVS): GISTEMP Global Temperature Anomalies (1880–2024), SVS Entry #5190.
  URL: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5190/
- NASA Visible Earth / Blue Marble: Global planetary day surface, ocean specular, normal, and cloud maps.
  URL: https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/ (Reto Stöckli, NASA Earth Observatory).
  License: Public Domain / MIT (Three.js asset repository).
- NASA Black Marble: Global nocturnal city lights composite (Suomi NPP VIIRS Day/Night Band).
  URL: https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/features/NightLights (NASA Earth Applied Sciences / GSFC; Román, M. O., et al., 2018).
  License: Public Domain (U.S. Government Work).
